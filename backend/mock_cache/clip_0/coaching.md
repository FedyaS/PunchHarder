## Guard drops on the cross — @2.188s-2.649s

The right hand opens too early during the cross, leaving the chin exposed. Keep the left hand glued to the face and rotate the hips fully before extending the right arm. This will maintain a tight guard and generate more power.

## Hip drive — @3.095s-3.238s

During the uppercut, the hips stay flat instead of driving upward. Engage the legs and thrust the hips forward to lift the punch from the ground. A strong hip drive adds height and snap to the uppercut.

## Balance and footwork — @4.299s-4.668s

The feet drift backward after the combination, breaking balance. Stay centered by keeping weight on the balls of the feet and shuffling lightly rather than stepping back. This will help you stay ready for the next exchange.
